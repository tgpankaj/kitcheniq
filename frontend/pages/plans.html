Frequently Asked Questions
Can I change plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

What happens if I exceed my order limit?

You will be notified at 80% usage. Once exceeded, new orders won't sync until next month or you upgrade.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes! Every paid plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from Settings. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing cycle.
